A New World

GTA VI takes place in Leonida, a fictional state inspired by Florida. Its world includes the famous Vice City and other surrounding regions.

👥

Two Main Characters

Follow Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as their lives become connected through a dangerous situation and a criminal conspiracy.

📱

Modern Culture

Rockstar's official character descriptions highlight viral videos, social media personalities and the internet culture of modern Leonida.

🎵

Music & Radio

Rockstar has confirmed in-game radio stations, soundtrack music and an original score.

🎮

Platforms

The announced launch platforms are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

🏙️

Return To Vice City

The game brings players back to a modern-day version of Vice City, expanding the setting beyond the city itself.

04 / THE NEXT GENERATION

GTA V vs GTA VI

How does the next Grand Theft Auto compare with its predecessor?

GTA V

SETTING

Los Santos and Blaine County, San Andreas.

PROTAGONISTS

Michael, Franklin and Trevor — three playable main characters.

WORLD

A fictional version of Southern California.

STORY

Three different lives become connected through a series of events.

ATMOSPHERE

Los Santos city life, countryside and the San Andreas landscape.

GTA VI

SETTING

Vice City and the wider state of Leonida.

PROTAGONISTS

Jason and Lucia — a central duo at the heart of the story.

WORLD

A fictional version of Florida, featuring cities, coastal regions and more.

STORY

Jason and Lucia navigate a criminal conspiracy and depend on each other.

ATMOSPHERE

Modern Vice City, neon nightlife, coastal scenery and internet culture.
